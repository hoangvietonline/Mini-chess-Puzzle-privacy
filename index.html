Mini Chess Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026
Developer: Khoaidev

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Chess Puzzle (the “Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Game.

Information We Handle

Game data stored on your device. The Game stores gameplay data locally on your device, including puzzle progress, coins and hint state, language, sound and vibration preferences, and any Remove Ads entitlement. We do not create player accounts and do not operate a server that receives this local gameplay data. You can remove it by clearing the Game’s app data or uninstalling the Game.

Advertising and privacy choices. When advertising is enabled, the Game uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and available ad services, these services may process IP address (which may estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, app and SDK diagnostic information, and interactions with the Game or advertisements.

We use UMP to present consent choices where required. Depending on your choices, you may still receive non-personalized or limited advertisements. You can use Privacy Choices in the Game’s Settings when it is available for your region, and can reset or delete your Android advertising ID through Android settings.

Analytics and crash data. The Game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. These services may automatically process game usage events, device and app information, installation identifiers, crash stack traces, relevant app state, and device metadata. Our current custom events cover actions such as starting or completing a puzzle, using a hint, and advertising or privacy events. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, message contents, or precise location through these custom events.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and save the Game experience.
  • To show, measure, and protect advertising when it is enabled.
  • To respect applicable consent and privacy choices.
  • To measure Game performance and usage, fix crashes and errors, and improve the Game.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or misuse.

Where required by law, we and our partners rely on your consent before using data for personalized advertising or other consent-based purposes.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. Information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention; Google User Messaging Platform for consent management; Firebase Analytics for usage analytics; Firebase Crashlytics for crash, error, and ANR reporting; and Google Play for app distribution and, if enabled in a future version, in-app purchase processing and restoration.

These providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. See how Google uses data when you use its partners’ sites or apps.

Retention and Security

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the Game’s data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google, Firebase, AdMob, or Google Play is retained according to the relevant service configuration, policies, and applicable law. We rely on service providers that use security measures, including encryption in transit where applicable; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or objection to certain processing of your personal information, or to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

For privacy questions or requests, use the Mini Chess Puzzle support page. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. We may need to verify a request and can assist only with information under our control. You may also use privacy tools offered by Google for information handled by Google, Firebase, AdMob, or Google Play.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user-generated content from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the support page above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy when the Game, its SDKs, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version at this URL and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
